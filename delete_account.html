Delete Your BabyQuil Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can delete your BabyQuil account directly from within the app, or by contacting us via email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and authentication details
  • Mother and baby profile data
  • All tracking data (feeding, sleep, nappy, health logs)
  • Photos, milestones, appointments and doctor notes
  • All cloud-synced data stored in Firebase

⚠️ This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (Recommended)

  1. Open BabyQuil
  2. Tap Settings (bottom navigation bar)
  3. Scroll down to Account
  4. Tap Delete account
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete forever

Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address linked to your BabyQuil account.

📧 appdeveloper049@gmail.com

We will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days of receiving your request.

Delete local data

To remove data stored on your device immediately, simply uninstall the BabyQuil app from your phone. This removes all locally stored data right away.
