Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

BabyQuil ("we", "our", or "the app") is a pregnancy and baby tracking application developed for parents. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

BabyQuil collects only the data you choose to enter. This includes:

  • Mother profile: name, age, blood type, doctor name, hospital, emergency contact, due date
  • Baby profile: name, date of birth, gender, birth weight and length
  • Pregnancy tracking: weekly notes, health logs (weight, blood pressure, symptoms), kick counter sessions, supplement records, bump photos
  • Baby tracking: feeding logs, sleep logs, nappy logs, daily care records, growth measurements, milestone records, baby photos
  • Appointments and doctor notes
  • Account information: email address (if you sign in with email/password), or your Google or Apple account identity (if you use those sign-in methods)

We do not collect any data automatically beyond what is necessary for the app to function (see Section 3).

2. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored in two ways:

  • On your device: A local copy of your data is stored using Hive, a lightweight database on your phone. The app works offline.
  • In the cloud: Your data is synced to Google Firebase Firestore, hosted on Google Cloud servers. This keeps your data safe if you change or lose your phone, and allows you to restore your data by signing back in to your account.

3. Authentication

BabyQuil requires you to create an account to use the app. You can sign in using:

  • Email address and password
  • Google account
  • Apple ID (iOS only)

Authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (provided by Google). If you sign in with email and password, we send a verification email to confirm your address. We do not store your password — it is managed securely by Firebase.

4. Photos

Photos you add (bump photos, baby photos) are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to any cloud server. If you uninstall the app, locally stored photos are removed from the app's storage.

5. Push Notifications

BabyQuil may send local notifications (e.g. appointment reminders). These are generated on your device and do not involve sending your data to a server. Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used for future remote notifications; no personal data is included in these messages.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes. Data is only shared with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging) — for account management and cloud sync
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google
  • Apple Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Apple
  • Google AdMob — to display advertisements (see Section 6a below)

These services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

6a. Advertising

BabyQuil is free to use and is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android) to serve relevant ads.
  • iOS users: When you first open the app, you will be shown an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission prompt. If you allow tracking, personalised ads may be shown. If you deny, non-personalised ads will be shown instead. Either choice does not affect app functionality.
  • We do not share your personal health or baby data with AdMob. Ad personalisation is based solely on your device's advertising identifier, not your baby or health records.
  • AdMob's data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

BabyQuil is designed for use by parents and caregivers. The app records information about babies and children, but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 directly.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time:

  • In-app deletion: Go to Profile → Delete Account. This immediately deletes your account and all associated cloud data permanently.
  • Local data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • By request: You may contact us at the email below to request deletion of your data. We will process your request within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit and Firebase security rules to ensure only you can access your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including: access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, and data portability. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
