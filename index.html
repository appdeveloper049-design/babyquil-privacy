Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

BabyQuil ("we", "our", or "the app") is a pregnancy and baby tracking application developed for personal use by parents. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

BabyQuil collects only the data you choose to enter. This includes:

  • Mother profile: name, age, blood type, doctor name, hospital, emergency contact, due date
  • Baby profile: name, date of birth, gender, birth weight and length
  • Pregnancy tracking: weekly notes, health logs (weight, blood pressure, symptoms), kick counter sessions, supplement records, bump photos
  • Baby tracking: feeding logs, sleep logs, nappy logs, daily care records, growth measurements, milestone records, baby photos
  • Appointments and doctor notes

We do not collect any data automatically beyond what is necessary for the app to function (see Section 3).

2. How We Store Your Data

Your data is stored in two ways:

  • On your device: All data is stored locally using Hive, a lightweight database on your phone. The app works fully offline.
  • In the cloud (optional): If you sign in or enable backup, your data is synced to Google Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage, hosted on Google Cloud servers. This allows your data to be available across devices and protected against phone loss.

3. Authentication

BabyQuil uses Firebase Authentication (provided by Google). On first launch, the app signs in anonymously to create a stable, private user identifier — no email or password is required. You may optionally sign in with email/password or a Google account to enable cloud backup and cross-device access.

4. Photos

Photos you add (bump photos, baby photos) are stored locally on your device. If cloud backup is enabled, photos are uploaded to Firebase Storage under your private user account. Photos are never shared publicly or with third parties.

5. Push Notifications

BabyQuil may send local notifications (e.g. supplement reminders, appointment alerts). These are generated on your device and do not involve sending your data to a server. Firebase Cloud Messaging may be used for future remote notifications; no personal data is included in these messages.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes. Data is only shared with the following service providers, solely to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Messaging) — for cloud sync and notifications
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google

Both services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

BabyQuil is designed for use by parents and caregivers. The app records information about babies and children, but is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 directly.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you use the app. You can delete your data at any time:

  • Local data: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Cloud data: You can request deletion of your cloud data by contacting us at the email below. We will delete your Firebase account and all associated data within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS/TLS encryption for data in transit and Firebase security rules to ensure only you can access your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including: access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, and data portability. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
